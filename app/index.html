    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions. Roughly 200 hours in total, by community estimates — about 50–60 hours for the story, the rest for side content, collectibles and cleanup. Play the stages in order: the story first, while the one missable is still in reach, then the world opens up for everything else.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable — and the rest of this page is a watch list of single-moment trophies, so you know where to keep a manual save. Nothing here is guesswork dressed up as fact: where a trigger is not yet confirmed in a published guide, this page says so.

      Before you play: two habits that save a platinum

      • Keep manual saves before big story missions. There is no mission replay for the Chapter 1 meditation spot, and several hidden trophies are tied to single story moments.
      • Do not use cheats. Turning on a cheat disables trophies, and you cannot save once one is used — the codes only unlock after the story in any case.
      • Meditate on Lucia's balcony in Chapter 1. It is the first of 18 meditation spots and the only one that can be lost. See Positive Mental Attitude below.

      Cheats disable trophies: Planet GTA 6, citing PowerPyx, GameSpot and PC Gamer.
